Calculation controls

Minimum governance controls required before production use.

Operating rules

  1. As-of date: every page must use one governed as-of timestamp and expose the data-through date.
  2. Filter consistency: product, region, segment and renewal-horizon filters must be applied to both numerator and denominator.
  3. Identity resolution: CRM account ID is the proposed master key; duplicate or merged accounts require an effective-dated crosswalk.
  4. Missing data: retain an “Unknown / Unscored” bucket; never replace missing values with zero without an approved rule.
  5. Currency: pipeline and retention metrics require a stated reporting currency and Finance-approved FX treatment.
  6. Versioning: retain model version, feature version, threshold version and calculation date with every score snapshot.
  7. Quality checks: validate freshness, row counts, uniqueness, referential integrity, band totals and reconciliation to Finance/CRM control totals.
  8. Access & privacy: apply least-privilege access, approved retention and suppression of sensitive free-text or personal data.
